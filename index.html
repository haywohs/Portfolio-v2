About me

A nonlinear career with a consistent operating principle

Understand the system. Improve the process. Protect the quality.

I am a software developer and QA professional based in Calgary. I completed SAIT’s Information Technology – Software Development diploma after working on product validation for Google Nest IoT devices.

Quality assurance taught me to investigate failures carefully, document reproducible evidence, and work across disciplines to improve product reliability. Software development gave me the tools to design the solution—not only identify the problem.

Today, I lead operations in a high-volume environment while continuing to build software. That combination has strengthened my judgment, communication, process discipline, and ability to remain calm when systems are under pressure.

I am pursuing software development, quality assurance, application support, and technical systems roles where reliability and continuous learning matter.
